## Questions

1. How does Nest resolve a provider (service) inside a controller?
2. What is `@Module({ imports, controllers, providers })` doing?

---

## Answers

1. Built-in Inversion of Control container and dependency injection.

Nest reads the controller's constructor parameters (types of explicit @Inject() tokens)

2. Provides metadata for organising nestjs project's internal strcuture.

Directly listed in its module's providers array. Exported by another module that is listed in this module's imports array.
